II — About

Who I am

I'm Say Varady, a developer based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. I spent the last few years as a Software Engineer at an HR SaaS startup — sitting in product meetings, running technical calls, and leading a team through implementation problems I used to solve myself. Somewhere in that process I noticed what I actually wanted more of was being hands-on again: writing the code, designing the system, solving the problem myself instead of delegating it.

So I've been moving deeper into backend and full-stack engineering, sharpening the technical edge that management pulled me away from, and building toward working with development teams beyond Cambodia.

Right now

Building this site as a record of that shift — the decisions I make and why, and the occasional lesson from moving between management and engineering.
