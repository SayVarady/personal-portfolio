About Me

Who Am I?

I'm Say Varady, a developer based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. I spent the last 3 years as a Software Engineer at an HR SaaS startup — sitting in product meetings, technical calls, and leading a team through complex implementation problems. Somewhere in that process I realized what I actually love is being hands-on: writing the code, designing the system, solving the problem myself — not just delegating it. So I'm doing something about it. I'm moving deeper into backend and full-stack engineering, sharpening the technical edge that management pulled me away from, and building toward working with development teams beyond Cambodia.

What I Do

I build full-stack web applications with Laravel and Vue, with 3 years of production experience shipping real features for an HR SaaS product used by real companies. I care about how systems are structured underneath — not just that they work, but why they're built the way they are.

What I'm Working Toward

I'm building this site as a record of that shift: the projects I'm building, the technical decisions I make and why, and the lessons from moving between management and engineering. If you're a developer or team working on interesting problems — especially across borders — I'd love to connect.
