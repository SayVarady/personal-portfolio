Why I Started Writing This Down

Idea Decision

For a long time this site was just a list: here are my skills, here are my projects, here's how to reach me. True, but thin. It read like a resume with better lighting. It didn't say anything about how any of it actually happened.

The Idea

The projects I'm proudest of were never straight lines. There was always a version one that got scrapped, a library I picked and later regretted, a night where I almost shipped the wrong architecture because it was the path of least resistance. None of that shows up in a project card with three tags and a "Live" link.

I wanted a place on this site for that part — the reasoning, not just the result. Somewhere to write down what I was actually thinking when I chose Laravel over something else, or why a "simple" feature took a week longer than planned. Not a polished engineering blog with best practices baked in — closer to a running log of a developer figuring things out in real time.

The Decision

I almost made this a generic "Blog" section — tutorials, how-tos, the usual. I killed that idea fast. I don't need another place competing with a thousand other tutorials on the same topics. What I do have that's actually mine is the specific, messy path from "product manager who missed writing code" to "engineer again," and every small decision along the way.

So Writing stayed narrow on purpose: every entry names an idea, a mistake, a decision, or a trade-off — sometimes more than one. If it doesn't fit one of those, it probably belongs somewhere else on the site, not here.

This entry is entry number one for exactly that reason. Everything else in this section should be able to point back to it and say: that's what this place is for.
